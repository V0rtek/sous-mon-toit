Eight ways to say “a choice is coming” a few seconds before it shows, so the one who has to choose is ready (eyes on the phone, thumb up) and the other player isn’t left waiting. Phone = the story is on the phone. Remote = the phone is black, the story is on the TV, so the cue has to reach someone who isn’t looking at it. Tap a phone to replay it with sound and buzz.
