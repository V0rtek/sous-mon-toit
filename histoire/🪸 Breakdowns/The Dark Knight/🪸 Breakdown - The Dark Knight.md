# 🪸 Breakdown — The Dark Knight (scene by scene)

Source : `Downloads/thedarkknight-screenplay.pdf` (Nolan, 141 p.).
The script has ~150 sluglines, grouped here into **52 dramatic scenes** (one scene = one question/action unit, even when it crosscuts).
2nd breakdown (story circle + intent of each scene, FR) : [[🪸 Story Circle - The Dark Knight]]

**Reading rules**
- **Macro** = matters to the whole story (the big goals and conflicts). Always at the top of each list.
- [micro] *= matters only inside the scene.*
- State format : `[audience/character] now [is/believes] [xyz]`.
- Macro loops are tagged by name: 🔓 opened · 🔁 brought back / progressed · ✅ paid off / closed. The chain shows every scene where the loop comes back, with the **current scene in bold**.

## 01 — Bank heist (opening)

Tone :
- Tense, clever, cold

Hooks used (curiosity / open loops started) :
- 🔓 **🃏 L1 JOKER**: who is he? The crew talks about him, nobody has seen him. (**1**, 4, 10, 14, 18, 19, 22, 24, 36, 37, 41, 43, 50)
- 🔓 (seed) **🧪 L13 EXPERIMENT**: "Criminals used to believe in things... What do you believe?" (**1**, 37, 41, 43, 47, 50, 51, 52)
- [micro] *Why does every clown have orders to kill another one?*
- [micro] *The silent alarm calls a private number. Whose bank is this?*

Surprises / Twists & Payoffs :
- 🔁 **🃏 L1**: Bozo IS the Joker. The boss was in the room the whole time.
- [micro] *Each clown kills the next one: a chain of betrayals.*
- [micro] *It's a mob bank (5,000 volts, a manager with a shotgun).*
- [micro] *The grenade only lets out red smoke.*
- [micro] *The bus melts into a line of school buses.*

Stakes + How they kept us engaged in that scene :
- A new player is robbing the mob itself. The criminal order is under attack.
- [micro] *Life or death every minute: the crew shrinks by one each beat.*
- [micro] *Hostages holding grenades with the pins pulled.*
- [micro] *The final reveal rewrites the whole scene.*

Beginning state :
- Audience now believes this is a normal crew with an absent boss.
- [micro] *Clowns now believe they'll split the money.*
- [micro] *Manager now believes the mob's name protects the bank.*

End state (what changed) :
- Audience now believes the Joker is smart, ruthless, and steals from the mob for fun.
- The mob now has been robbed by someone who fears nothing.
- [micro] *Audience now knows the Joker hides in plain sight (mask, bus line).*
- [micro] *Manager now is alive and humiliated.*

Relevant choices made (+ the stakes linked to em) :
- The Joker robs the mob → he declares war on the criminal order. Stake: everything that follows.
- [micro] *The Joker has his crew kill each other → all the money, no witnesses.*
- [micro] *The manager fights back → his life.*
- [micro] *The Joker spares the manager → a witness to spread the story.*
