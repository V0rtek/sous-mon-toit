# 🪸 What Happens — The Dark Knight

3rd breakdown. High level only: what happens, and what each scene leaves behind. Micro stuff is cut.
Same scene numbers as [[🪸 Breakdown - The Dark Knight]] and [[🪸 Story Circle - The Dark Knight]].

**What a scene can leave behind (4 outputs)**
- 🌱 **Seed**: planted now, pays later. Either a belief that will be flipped, or something we'll care about losing. `(→ n)` = the scene where it pays.
- ❓ **Opens**: a question the audience now asks.
- 💡 **Reveal**: answers an open question, or flips a seed. `(← n)` = where it was opened/seeded.
- 🔄 **Change**: can't go back. A choice, a death, a capture, an alliance made or broken, someone crossing a line.

---

**01 — Bank heist**
- Clowns rob a mob bank. Each one kills the next, on the boss's orders.
- The last clown takes off his mask: it's the Joker. He escapes in a line of school buses.
- 🔄 The Joker has robbed the mob.
- ❓ Who is the Joker, what does he want?
- 🌱 He plans several moves ahead and sacrifices his own men (→ 38: he planned his own capture).

**02 — Police roof**
- The police light the bat-signal. On TV: is a masked vigilante legal?
- Gordon waits on the roof instead of going home. Ramirez (a detective) has her mother in hospital.
- 🌱 Batman is officially hunted, secretly used by the police (→ 52).
- 🌱 Ramirez needs money (→ 48: why she's dirty).

**03 — Garage**
- Batman breaks up a drug deal. Fake "Batmen" with guns are there too.
- He arrests everyone and tells the fakes to stay home.
- 🌱 Batman has armed imitators (→ 19: one becomes the Joker's first victim).

**04 — Robbed bank, night**
- The robbed bank held mob money. Batman marked bills: 5 banks hold the mob's cash.
- Plan: raid all 5 at once. The Joker "can wait." The new DA, Harvey Dent, will want in.
- 🔄 Plan set: take the mob's money.
- ❓ Can they break the mob?
- 🌱 The heroes underestimate the Joker (→ 18 onward).

**05 — Bunker**
- Alfred stitches Bruce's wounds. Bruce studies Dent on his screens. Dent dates Rachel, the woman Bruce loves.
- 🌱 Bruce wants a way out of being Batman (→ 9, 20, 31).
- ❓ Rachel: Bruce or Dent?

**06 — Courtroom**
- Dent prosecutes mob boss Maroni. A witness pulls a gun on Dent, it jams, Dent knocks him out.
- Dent flips a coin to decide who leads: "I make my own luck."
- 🌱 Dent is brave and clean, we like him (→ 38–51: his fall).
- ❓ Is the coin luck or rigged? (→ 34)

**07 — DA's office**
- Dent wants in on the raids. Gordon distrusts Dent's office; Dent distrusts Gordon's cops (he investigated some, Ramirez included).
- Deal: Dent signs the warrants.
- 🔄 Gordon and Dent work together.
- ❓ Who is dirty in Gordon's unit?
- 🌱 The cops have a nickname for Dent (→ 40: "Two-Face").

**08 — Wayne Enterprises**
- Bruce cancels a deal with Lau, a Chinese businessman. He knew Lau launders mob money.
- Reese, a company lawyer, starts digging.
- 🌱 Lau holds the mob's money (→ 10).
- 🌱 Reese digs in the archives (→ 26).

**09 — Restaurant**
- Bruce joins Rachel and Dent's dinner. Dent: "you die a hero, or live long enough to become the villain."
- Bruce sees Dent could replace Batman and offers to fund him.
- 🔄 Bruce backs Dent, his rival for Rachel, as Batman's successor.
- 🌱 "Die a hero / become the villain" (→ 52).

**10 — Mob meeting + raids**
- Lau tells the mob bosses he moved all their money to one secret place and is flying to Hong Kong.
- The police raid the 5 banks: empty. Someone warned the mob.
- The Joker walks in and offers to kill Batman for half the money. Refused. A bounty is put on him.
- 🔄 The mob's money is out of reach, with Lau.
- 💡 There's a leak in the police (← 7).
- 🌱 The audience believes the Joker wants Batman dead (→ 37: he wants him alive).

**11 — Roof: Dent meets Batman**
- Dent and Gordon blame each other for the leak.
- Batman: if I bring Lau back, can you make him talk? Dent: yes. "I knew the risks."
- 🔄 The three commit to bringing Lau back.
- 🌱 "We knew the risks" (→ 51).

**12 — Gadgets**
- Fox gives Bruce a lighter suit and a way to be snatched by a plane.
- 🌱 Blades that fire from the gauntlets (→ 50: how Batman beats the Joker).

**13 — Ballet / yacht**
- Bruce's alibi: he flew off with the whole ballet company.
- (no long-term output)

**14 — Pool hall**
- Bounty hunters deliver the Joker's "body" to Gambol, a mob boss. The Joker gets up, kills Gambol, takes his men.
- He tells how he got his scars: an abusive father.
- 🔄 The Joker has a gang.
- 🌱 Scar story #1 (→ 22: he tells a different one).

**15 — Hong Kong lunch**
- Fox cancels the deal with Lau in person and leaves a phone in Lau's building that maps its insides.
- 🌱 A phone can map a building (→ 45: the whole city).

**16 — Hong Kong extraction**
- Batman breaks into Lau's tower. A plane snatches both of them off the roof.
- 🔄 Lau is captured.

**17 — Lau talks**
- Lau will give up his clients. Their money was pooled, so Dent can charge the whole mob as one conspiracy.
- Gordon keeps Lau in his own holding cells.
- 🔄 There's a case against the whole mob.
- 🌱 Lau is held at Gordon's station (→ 38: the Joker gets locked up there on purpose).

**18 — Mass arrests**
- 549 criminals arrested. The Mayor to Dent: if they find anything dirty on you, they all walk.
- The mob hires the Joker.
- A dead fake Batman hangs outside City Hall: "Will the real Batman please stand up?"
- 🔄 The mob is in court, and has hired the Joker.
- 🌱 The whole case depends on Dent staying clean (→ 52: the cover-up).

**19 — The video**
- On TV: the Joker tortures the fake Batman from the garage. Demand: Batman unmasks and turns himself in, or people die every day.
- ❓ Will Batman unmask? Deadline: one death a day.
- 🌱 Reinforces: the Joker wants Batman gone (→ 37).

**20 — Fundraiser**
- Bruce toasts Dent for real: "I believe in Harvey Dent."
- On the balcony, Bruce tells Rachel the day he quits is close. She says don't ask her to wait.
- The police find 3 names on the Joker's card: a judge, the police commissioner, Dent.
- ❓ Will the 3 targets survive?
- 🌱 Bruce believes he'll soon quit and be with Rachel (→ 39).

**21 — Judge and commissioner**
- The judge dies in a car bomb. The commissioner is poisoned in his guarded office.
- 🔄 Two of the three targets are dead.
- ❓ Dent is next.

**22 — The party**
- Dent proposes. Rachel has no answer.
- The Joker crashes the party looking for Dent. Bruce hides Dent, comes back as Batman.
- The Joker tells a different scar story, drops Rachel out a window. Batman dives and saves her.
- 💡 The scar stories contradict: the Joker lies about himself (← 14).
- 🌱 The Joker sees Batman will risk everything for Rachel (→ 37).

**23 — Police, morning**
- The cops say the case is dead: nobody will testify. Dent refuses to quit and forces Lau to testify.
- 🔄 Dent keeps the case alive.

**24 — Alfred's story**
- A bandit stole jewels and threw them away. "Some men just want to watch the world burn."
- 💡 The Joker doesn't want money (half answer, ← 1).
- ❓ How did they catch the bandit? (→ 39)

**25 — Two dead men**
- Two dead men named Harvey and Dent. Next target: the Mayor, at the commissioner's funeral.
- Batman pulls a fingerprint from a bullet.
- ❓ Will the Mayor survive the funeral parade?

**26 — Reese**
- Reese found old blueprints: Bruce Wayne is Batman. He wants $10M a year. Fox calls his bluff and Reese backs off.
- ❓ Will Reese expose Batman? (→ 42)
- 🌱 Bruce's R&D is building something secret (→ 45).

**27 — Fingerprint**
- The print leads to an apartment overlooking the parade.
- (no long-term output: it's the bridge to 28)

**28 — Funeral parade**
- The honor guard is the Joker's men. They shoot at the Mayor. Gordon takes the bullets and seems dead.
- Dent finds a shooter whose name tag reads "Rachel Dawes", and kidnaps him.
- 🔄 Gordon is "dead".
- 🔄 Dent breaks the rules (kidnaps a suspect).
- ❓ Rachel is the next target.

**29 — Gordon's house**
- Gordon's wife screams at Batman, hidden in the dark: "You brought this on us." Their son sees Batman.
- 🌱 Gordon's family, the son especially (→ 51).

**30 — Maroni**
- Batman drops Maroni off a fire escape. Maroni knows nothing: nobody will give up the Joker. "Take off your mask."
- 🔄 Batman's fear doesn't work anymore.

**31 — Dent and the coin**
- Dent sends Rachel to hide at Bruce's. In a basement, he flips a coin for the kidnapped shooter's life.
- Batman stops him: if anyone saw this, the case dies. Batman says he'll turn himself in.
- 🔄 Batman decides to unmask.
- 🌱 Batman hides Dent's dark side (→ 52).
- 🌱 Dent will kill on a coin flip (→ 43, 44).

**32 — Bruce and Rachel**
- Rachel says yes: when he quits, they'll be together. They kiss.
- Bruce and Alfred burn every piece of evidence. Alfred: "Endure. Let them hate you." Bruce refuses.
- 🔄 Bruce is ready to surrender.
- 🌱 Batman could be the one everyone hates (→ 52).

**33 — Press conference**
- The crowd demands Batman. As Bruce stands up, Dent says "I am the Batman" and gets arrested.
- 💡 Batman doesn't unmask (← 19).
- 🔄 Dent is bait for the Joker.

**34 — Letter and coin**
- Rachel gives Alfred a letter for Bruce, "when the time is right."
- She sees Dent's coin has two heads.
- ❓ What's in the letter?
- 💡 The coin is rigged (← 6).

**35 — Convoy chase**
- The Joker attacks Dent's convoy. Batman's car is wrecked; he goes on with a bike.
- The Joker dares Batman to run him over. Batman crashes instead. The armored truck's driver arrests the Joker: it's Gordon, alive.
- 🔄 The Joker is caught.
- 💡 Gordon is alive (← 28).
- 💡 Batman won't kill, not even the Joker.

**36 — False calm**
- Gordon is made commissioner and goes home. A Joker thug in a cell has stomach pain.
- Dent leaves in a car with two of Gordon's cops.
- 🌱 The thug's belly (→ 38: a phone bomb).
- 🌱 Dent left with Gordon's cops (→ 37, 44).

**37 — Interrogation**
- Dent never got home. Batman beats the Joker for answers.
- The Joker: he doesn't want to kill Batman, he needs him.
- Dent and Rachel are tied to bombs at two addresses. Batman can save one. He goes for Rachel.
- 💡 The Joker doesn't want Batman dead (← 10, 19).
- 🔄 Batman chooses Rachel over Dent.
- ❓ Who will be saved?

**38 — The two bombs**
- The Joker swapped the addresses: Batman finds Dent. Rachel dies, after telling Dent "yes". Dent burns on half his face.
- The thug's belly holds a phone bomb. The station blows open and the Joker escapes with Lau.
- 💡 The capture was the Joker's plan (← 1, 17).
- 🔄 Rachel is dead. Dent is disfigured. The Joker is free, with Lau.

**39 — Aftermath**
- Alfred reads Rachel's letter: she chose Dent. Bruce doesn't know. Alfred keeps it.
- Alfred: they caught the bandit by burning the whole forest.
- 💡 Rachel chose Dent (← 5), for the audience only.
- ❓ Will Bruce ever learn it? (→ 52)
- 🌱 To win, Batman will go to extremes (→ 45).

**40 — Hospital**
- Dent refuses treatment and makes Gordon say his nickname: "Two-Face."
- Maroni offers Gordon the Joker's location.
- 💡 The nickname (← 7).
- ❓ What will Dent do?

**41 — The money burns**
- The Joker burns his half of the mob's money and has the Chechen (a mob boss) killed.
- 💡 He wants chaos, not money (← 1).
- 🔄 The Joker runs organized crime.

**42 — Reese on TV**
- Reese is about to name Batman on TV. The Joker calls: kill Reese within an hour, or a hospital blows up.
- 💡 The Joker protects Batman's identity, he needs him (← 37).
- ❓ Will someone kill Reese? Which hospital?

**43 — Reese saved / Dent freed**
- Bruce rams his car into a truck aimed at Reese's police van. Reese, saved, keeps quiet.
- At the hospital, the Joker frees Dent, gives him a gun. Dent flips for the Joker's life: he lives. The hospital explodes.
- 💡 Reese won't talk (← 26).
- 🔄 Dent is loose, with a gun and the coin.

**44 — Bar**
- Dent finds Wuertz, the cop who handed him over. The coin lands burned side up: he kills him.
- 🔄 Dent has killed a cop.
- ❓ Who's the other dirty cop, the one who took Rachel?

**45 — The machine**
- Batman shows Fox a machine that turns every phone in the city into a microphone. Fox helps once, then quits.
- The Joker on TV: by nightfall the city is his. Get out.
- 🔄 Batman spies on the whole city.
- 💡 The R&D secret (← 26).
- ❓ What happens at nightfall?

**46 — Evacuation + Maroni**
- The city flees: civilians on one ferry, prisoners on another.
- Maroni tells Dent it was Ramirez. The coin spares Maroni, kills his driver.
- 💡 Ramirez is the other dirty cop (← 44).

**47 — The ferries**
- Both ferries are full of explosives. Each holds the trigger for the other. At midnight both blow, unless one blows the other first.
- ❓ Will one boat blow up the other?

**48 — Ramirez**
- At gunpoint, Ramirez lures Gordon's wife and kids to where Rachel died. The coin spares her.
- 💡 She was bought because of her mother's hospital bills (← 2).
- ❓ Will Dent kill Gordon's family?

**49 — The Joker's building**
- The machine finds the Joker. The "clowns" in the windows are hostages; the "doctors" are his men.
- Batman fights off the police team to stop them shooting hostages. Gordon learns Dent has his family.
- 🔄 Batman goes against the police.

**50 — Ferry votes + Joker caught**
- The civilians vote to blow the prisoners, but nobody presses. A prisoner throws his trigger out the window. Midnight passes.
- Batman beats the Joker and saves him from a fall. The Joker: his real card is Harvey Dent.
- 💡 People don't turn on each other (← 47).
- 🔄 The Joker is caught for good.
- ❓ What did he do to Dent? (→ 51)

**51 — Burned building**
- Dent holds Gordon's son at gunpoint and flips: he shoots Batman, spares himself, aims at the boy.
- Batman tackles Dent. Dent falls and dies. The boy is saved.
- 🔄 Dent is dead. The boy is alive.
- ❓ If the city learns what Dent did, his convictions collapse (← 18).

**52 — Ending**
- Batman takes the blame for Dent's murders, so Dent stays a hero and the criminals stay locked up.
- Gordon smashes the bat-signal and starts hunting Batman. Alfred burns Rachel's letter. Fox destroys the machine.
- 🔄 Batman is the city's villain, by choice.
- 💡 "Die a hero / become the villain" (← 9).
- 💡 Bruce will never know about the letter (← 39).

**Count:** 50 of 52 scenes leave at least one output. The 2 that don't (13, 27) are a gag and a bridge.
