
# 🪸 One Act — The Dark Knight

The Dark Knight rebuilt as **one act, 12 scenes, one circle**. Same format as [[🪸 What Happens - The Dark Knight]].
The station of the circle is next to each scene title.

**Outputs**
- 🌱 **Seed**: planted now, pays later. A belief that will be flipped, or something we'll care about losing. `(→ n)` = where it pays.
- ❓ **Opens**: a question the audience now asks.
- 💡 **Reveal**: answers a question, or flips a seed. `(← n)` = where it was opened/seeded.
- 🔄 **Change**: can't go back. A choice, a death, a capture, an alliance made or broken.

**The circle in one line:** Batman wants to quit by handing the city to Dent → the Joker forces him to choose between his mask and people's lives → he gives up, catches the Joker anyway, and that capture costs him Rachel and Dent → he ends up the one man who can never quit.

**The two choices:**
- Scene 6: he gives up the mask (his lie: if I stop, the killing stops and I get Rachel).
- Scene 12: he takes the blame forever (the truth: Batman is the one who can be hated).

---

**01 — Bank heist** · YOU
- Clowns rob a bank that hides mob money. Each one kills the next, on the boss's orders.
- The last one takes off his mask: the Joker. He drives off with the money.
- 🔄 Someone is robbing the mob.
- ❓ Who is the Joker, what does he want?
- 🌱 He plans several moves ahead and uses his own men as pieces (→ 9: his capture was part of the plan).

**02 — Police roof** · YOU
- Batman, Gordon (head of the police unit) and Harvey Dent (the DA) meet on the roof. Thanks to them, 549 mobsters were charged today.
- Gordon: the convictions hold only as long as Dent's name stays clean. Dent: he knew the risks when he took the job.
- Ramirez, one of Gordon's detectives, asks him for a pay advance: her mother's hospital bills.
- 🔄 The mob is in court. The three are a team.
- 🌱 The case dies if Dent is ever seen dirty (→ 12: the cover-up).
- 🌱 Dent knew the risks (→ 12: "we all chose this").
- 🌱 Ramirez needs money (→ 11: she sold Dent and Rachel).

**03 — Dinner, then balcony** · NEED
- Bruce Wayne joins Rachel, the woman he loves, and Dent, her boyfriend. Dent defends Batman: men like that either die heroes or last long enough to become villains.
- Dent flips a coin to decide who pays the bill. He says he makes his own luck.
- On the balcony, Bruce tells Rachel that Dent can replace Batman, and when Batman stops, they can be together. She doesn't say no.
- 🔄 Bruce chooses Dent as his successor.
- ❓ Rachel: Bruce or Dent?
- 🌱 Hero or villain (→ 12).
- 🌱 Dent's coin (→ 7: it has two heads).
- 🌱 Bruce believes Rachel will wait for him (→ 10: she chose Dent).

**04 — The video** · GO
- The jailed mob bosses have hired the Joker. On every TV, he films a man in a Batman costume, beaten, then killed.
- His demand: Batman unmasks and turns himself in. Until he does, someone dies every day.
- That night the judge on the mob case dies in a car bomb. The police commissioner is poisoned at his desk.
- 🔄 Two officials are dead.
- ❓ Will Batman unmask?
- ❓ Dent is the third name on the Joker's list.
- 🌱 The audience believes the Joker wants Batman gone (→ 9: he needs him).

**05 — The fundraiser** · SEARCH
- Bruce throws Dent a fundraiser. Dent proposes to Rachel. She has no answer.
- The Joker crashes the party looking for Dent. Bruce hides Dent in a closet and comes back as Batman.
- The Joker throws Rachel out a window. Batman jumps after her and saves her. The Joker escapes.
- 🔄 Dent has proposed. Rachel hasn't answered.
- 🌱 The Joker sees that Batman will risk everything for Rachel (→ 9).

**06 — Batman gives up** · SEARCH (choice 1)
- Dent kidnaps one of the Joker's men and flips his coin for the man's life. Batman stops him: if anyone saw this, every conviction falls.
- Batman decides to turn himself in tomorrow. In the hideout, he and Alfred burn every piece of evidence.
- Alfred: stay Batman, let them hate you, that's what he's for. Bruce refuses.
- 🔄 Batman decides to unmask.
- 🌱 Batman hides Dent's dark side (→ 12).
- 🌱 Dent can kill on a coin flip (→ 11).
- 🌱 Batman could be the one everyone hates (→ 12).

**07 — Press conference** · SEARCH
- Before it starts, Rachel gives Alfred a letter for Bruce, "for the right time."
- The crowd demands Batman. As Bruce stands up, Dent says he is Batman and is arrested in his place, as bait for the Joker.
- Dent tosses Rachel his coin: it has two heads.
- 🔄 Dent is bait.
- 💡 The coin is rigged: Dent never left anything to chance (← 3).
- ❓ What's in the letter?

**08 — The convoy** · FIND
- The Joker attacks the truck carrying Dent. He stands in the street and dares Batman to run him over. Batman swerves and crashes. Gordon arrests the Joker.
- Ramirez offers to drive Dent and Rachel home.
- Gordon goes home. His son asks if Batman saved him.
- 🔄 The Joker is caught.
- 💡 Batman won't kill, not even the Joker.
- 🌱 Ramirez drives Dent and Rachel (→ 9).
- 🌱 Gordon's son (→ 12).

**09 — Interrogation** · TAKE
- Dent and Rachel never got home. Batman beats the Joker for answers.
- The Joker: he doesn't want Batman dead, he wants to watch him break his one rule.
- Dent and Rachel are tied to bombs at two addresses, minutes left. Batman can reach only one. He goes for Rachel.
- 💡 The Joker doesn't want Batman dead (← 4).
- 💡 The capture was the plan: while the police celebrated, his cop inside took Dent and Rachel (← 1, 8).
- 🔄 Batman chooses Rachel over Dent.
- ❓ Who will be saved?

**10 — The two bombs** · TAKE
- The Joker swapped the addresses: Batman finds Dent. Rachel dies on the phone with Dent, after telling him yes. Dent burns on half his face.
- At dawn, Alfred opens Rachel's letter: she chose Dent, not Bruce. He keeps it from Bruce.
- 🔄 Rachel is dead. Dent is disfigured.
- 💡 Rachel chose Dent (← 3), for the audience only.
- ❓ Will Bruce ever find out?

**11 — Dent visits the Joker** · RETURN
- Dent leaves the hospital and walks into the Joker's cell with a gun.
- The Joker: it was Gordon's own detective, Ramirez, who handed them over. The people with plans did this; only chance is fair.
- Dent flips his coin, now burned on one side. The Joker lives. Dent leaves to make Gordon's people pay.
- 💡 Ramirez sold them (← 2, 8).
- 🔄 Dent turns: from now on, the coin decides who lives.
- ❓ What will Dent do to Gordon?

**12 — The burned building** · RETURN → CHANGE (choice 2)
- Ramirez, at gunpoint, has lured Gordon's family to where Rachel died. Dent flips for each of them: he shoots Batman, spares himself, aims at Gordon's son.
- Batman tells him they chose this together, all three. Then he tackles Dent. Dent falls and dies. The boy is saved.
- Gordon: if the city learns what Dent did, every mobster walks.
- Batman takes Dent's murders on himself. Dent stays a hero. Gordon smashes the bat-signal and starts hunting Batman. Alfred burns Rachel's letter.
- 🔄 Dent is dead. The boy is alive.
- 🔄 Batman becomes the city's villain, by choice.
- 💡 Hero or villain: Dent died a hero, Batman lives on as the villain (← 3).
- 💡 The cover-up protects the convictions (← 2, 6).
- 💡 "Let them hate you" (← 6).
- 💡 Bruce will never know about the letter (← 10).

## Cut, and what it cost

| Cut | Why it could go | What it cost |
|---|---|---|
| Lau, Hong Kong, the RICO case | A whole act of its own: "can he break the mob?" | The mob falls offscreen, in one line (scene 2) |
| Reese, who knows Batman's identity | A side act with no change to Batman | Nothing the main story needed |
| The ferries | The Joker's test of the whole city | ⚠️ **The hopeful half of the answer.** In the film, the people pass the test and Dent fails it. Here only Dent is tested, so the ending is darker: order holds only because Batman lies |
| The phone-spying machine | Batman crossing a line to find the Joker | Not needed: the Joker never escapes |
| Gordon's fake death | A mid-film surprise | One reveal fewer, but Gordon's son still carries the climax |
| Gambol, Maroni, Scarecrow, the parade | Separate mob intrigues | — |
| The Joker's escape | The film needed it to keep going | Here the Joker wins from his cell, through Dent |
