It's a tragedy of a man's moral downfall. Early in the story we meet Michael Corleone, an idealistic young man determined to stay above the murky world of his father's criminal activities and make a legitimate contribution to society.

The fatal flaw in Michael's character is his unquestioning love for his father. He steps in, to save dad from assassination in the hospital, and in doing so enters his father's world.

Once he lands on the sticky paper, he is drawn deeper and deeper into the criminal life of the family, until at the end he has become his father, a fully committed gangster for life. Like Macbeth, Hamlet, Mark Anthony and others, Michael is brought down by a flaw in his character that sets a series of events in play, resulting in his moral destruction.
